## 2. Meditation ("The Path · Meditation 101")

**Eyebrow:** The Path · Meditation 101
**Heading:** Explained simply — and scientifically
**Sub:** You don't do meditation. You sit, you surrender, and it happens.

### How to meditate (5 steps)

1. **Sit cross-legged, hands joined or at rest** — Any comfortable seat
   will do — floor or chair. Cross the legs and rest the palms open on the
   knees, or join the hands together in the lap; either closes the
   circuit. Keep the spine easy and upright.
2. **Close the eyes** — Nothing to look at. The world can wait an hour.
3. **Watch the breath** — Don't control it. Just notice it arriving and
   leaving.
4. **Let the thoughts flow** — They are traffic, not enemies. Watching
   them is the practice.
5. **Surrender and observe** — Surrender means: I am nothing. Let the
   universe — nature, God, whichever word is yours — deal with it. Stop
   trying to meditate and allow it to happen to you.

> That's the whole method. Nothing to buy, nothing to master.

Photo caption: "The posture — nothing more than this."

### Quick answers

- **Best time → Sunrise** — Brahmamuhurtham is finest — but no hour is
  forbidden. The one you keep is the right one.
- **How long → It happens, you don't do it** — Beginners: start with 30
  minutes, or an hour if it comes easily. Some sit 1–2 hours; some drop
  deep in minutes. Comparison is a thief of joy.
- **The goal → There isn't one** — Keep sitting. Life reorders itself —
  physically, emotionally, spiritually. Try it and watch.

## 5. How it's built (the "way" it works)

- **Not static HTML pages per section.** `index (3).html` is a single
  page app: one big HTML template with `<sc-if>` / `<sc-for>` custom tags
  (a lightweight component/templating syntax — looks like it's rendered by
  the "DeckStage" runtime, `deck-stage.js` / `support.js`) that show or
  hide "views" (`viewHome`, `viewPractice` = Meditation, `viewEvents`,
  `viewGuru` = About Me, `viewMission`, `viewWisdom`, `viewHealth`, etc.)
  based on which nav item was clicked. Navigating just swaps which
  `<sc-if>` block is visible — no page reload.
- **Content lives in JavaScript, not the HTML markup.** All the real
  copy — the "About Me" bio, the meditation steps, the testimonials, the
  gathering dates — is defined as plain JS arrays/objects near the bottom
  of `index (3).html` (inside a `<script type="text/x-dc">` block, in a
  `renderVals()`/data method), e.g. `guruStrengths`, `floatVoices`,
  `howSteps`, `quickAnswers`, `deepCards`, `sessions()`, `GATHERINGS`. The
  `{{ ... }}` placeholders in the HTML just pull these values in at render
  time.
- **Events are date-driven, not hand-curated per page.** The `GATHERINGS`
  array holds `{ day, month, year, title, meta, ends }`; a small
  `splitGatherings()` function automatically buckets each entry into
  "upcoming" or "past" by comparing `ends` to `Date.now()`, so the page
  doesn't need manual editing once an event's end time passes.
  Similarly `sessions()` computes each visitor's local time for the daily
  IST sessions on the fly.
- **Multi-language, but only for short UI strings.** `gaw-i18n.js`
  (`window.GAW_STRINGS`) and `gaw-phrases.js` hold Hindi/Telugu/Kannada/
  Tamil translations for interface labels (nav items, buttons, headings
  like "About Me" → "परिचय"). The long-form teaching text (the meditation
  instructions, the guru bio, testimonials) is intentionally **English
  only for now** — there's a comment in `gaw-i18n.js` explicitly saying
  long passages stay English until a native speaker reviews translations,
  since "a wrong word in a spiritual text is worse than an English one."
- **The Mass Meditation ("Global Peace") experience is its own page**
  (`mass-meditation.html`), separate from the Events tab inside the main
  app — it's a focused landing page for the daily 8:30 PM sit, with its
  own countdown logic, calendar export, pledge form, and city-group
  directory, sharing the same visual language and `sc-if`/`sc-for`
  templating approach.
